THE DATASET

Four terms you need first

DNA

The instruction manual in every cell, written in 4 letters: A, C, G, T.

Gene

One section of DNA that tells the body how to make one part.

Variant

A change in the DNA letters, like a typo in a book. Some are harmless, some cause disease.

Genome build

A standard “map” of human DNA. GRCh37 is the old map, GRCh38 the new one.

INSIGHTS

Key takeaways

55% unknown

Most variants are still uncertain, and that pile grows every year.

Little double-checking

77% come from one lab; only 0.5% were expert-reviewed.

Disagreement where it matters

BRCA1/2, the most-tested genes, have the most conflicts.

Cleaning was essential

Without it, every number would be doubled or wrong.

Why it matters: clearer data means clearer test results for patients, better-aimed research, and knowing which labels to trust.
